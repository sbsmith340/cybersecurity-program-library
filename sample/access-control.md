## Control statements

**AC-1 Policy and Procedures.** The organization maintains an approved access control policy and supporting procedures, reviews them at least annually, and assigns an owner responsible for their upkeep and communication.

**AC-2 Account Management.** Every account (user, privileged, service, shared, guest) has a documented owner and purpose, is approved before creation, is reviewed periodically, and is disabled promptly on role change, departure or inactivity.

**AC-3 Access Enforcement.** Systems enforce approved authorizations for access to information and functions, using role-based or attribute-based rules that default to deny.

**AC-4 Information Flow Enforcement.** Approved flows of sensitive information between systems, network segments and external parties are defined and enforced through technical controls such as firewalls, gateways and data loss prevention rules.

**AC-5 Separation of Duties.** Duties that could enable fraud or undetected misuse, such as requesting and approving access or developing and releasing code, are assigned to different people and enforced by system roles.

**AC-6 Least Privilege.** Users and processes receive only the access needed for their tasks. Administrative rights are separate from daily accounts, are time-bound where practical, and are reviewed regularly.

**AC-7 Unsuccessful Logon Attempts.** Systems limit consecutive failed logon attempts and apply a lockout or delay, with alerting on repeated failures against sensitive accounts.

**AC-8 System Use Notification.** Systems that process sensitive information display an approved notice before access stating authorized use, monitoring and consequences of misuse, and require acknowledgment where appropriate.

**AC-11 Device Lock.** Endpoints and sessions lock automatically after a defined period of inactivity and require re-authentication to resume.

**AC-12 Session Termination.** Sessions on applications and remote connections end automatically after defined inactivity or time limits, or on user logout.

**AC-14 Permitted Actions Without Identification or Authentication.** Actions allowed without authentication, such as viewing public web content, are explicitly listed, approved and kept free of sensitive data.

**AC-17 Remote Access.** Remote access methods are authorized, use encrypted channels and multi-factor authentication, are routed through managed access points, and are monitored.

**AC-18 Wireless Access.** Wireless networks are authorized, use strong authentication and encryption, and are separated from sensitive internal segments. Rogue access points are detected.

**AC-19 Access Control for Mobile Devices.** Mobile devices that access organizational data are enrolled in device management, encrypted, protected by a passcode, and can be locked or wiped when lost.

**AC-20 Use of External Systems.** Use of external systems, including personal devices and third-party platforms, to process or store organizational data is limited to approved cases with defined terms and technical restrictions.

**AC-21 Information Sharing.** Sharing of sensitive information with external parties requires a documented business need, an authorization decision by the information owner, and an approved sharing method.

**AC-22 Publicly Accessible Content.** Only designated, trained personnel may post to public systems. Content is reviewed for non-public information before posting and checked periodically.
